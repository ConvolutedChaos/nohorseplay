Cruise is the plain speedometer. Engine turns the dial into a tachometer and runs a simulated V8 and automatic transmission off your real speed. Cluster shows both dials at once with a mechanical odometer under them — tap the trip drums to zero them. Hold the gas pedal — bottom right, or the space bar — to rev it while you're stopped; drag down from where you pressed to feather the throttle.
